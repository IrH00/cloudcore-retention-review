How to use this demonstration & model limitations

Open an account card to see its evidence. Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on a Mac) to find a customer by name or ID.

  • The final model scores the same 280 accounts used to train it; those individual scores are in-sample, not independent predictions. The metrics above use stratified 10-fold out-of-fold validation. Precision and recall use a 0.50 threshold.
  • The historical label combines customers who churned and those flagged at risk. A score is a review signal, not a confirmed probability of future churn.
  • Model inputs: industry, support tier, tenure, monthly recurring revenue and product count. Region, identifiers and raw product mix are excluded as inputs; the historical label is the training target.
  • Usage and billing are a single May 2026 snapshot, not a trend. Tickets and reviews are historical extracts, not current account status.
